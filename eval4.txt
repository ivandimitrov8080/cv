Stronger resumes typically include quantifiable, result-oriented metrics for all relevant roles. For example, while your work at
Deutsche Börse AG highlights impressive achievements such as reducing GLEIF import time from 5 hours to 10 minutes, similar
quantifiable outcomes are not provided for your roles at IDimitrov Ltd. and RA Creative. Consider adding specific metrics—such
as project delivery timelines, client satisfaction scores, or measurable improvements in system performance—to these positions

We would have expected to see more explicit benchmarks, goals, or comparisons to previous performance across all recent
roles. While you mention reducing production defects and improving import times at Deutsche Börse AG, other roles like
IDimitrov Ltd. and RA Creative do not specify project outcomes relative to targets or prior states. You might enhance your
resume by including statements such as “improved client onboarding speed by X% compared to previous process” or
“delivered projects Y% faster than industry average.”

For leadership or managerial responsibilities, measurable achievements such as team growth, project success rates, or
employee development are not clearly quantified. At Deutsche Börse AG, you note recruiting and onboarding a senior
developer but do not specify outcomes like reduced ramp-up time or improved team productivity. Including these details would
better showcase your effectiveness in leadership roles.

Although you reference transaction volumes and some integration achievements, relevant industry-specific KPIs or metrics
(such as customer retention rates, lead conversion rates, or uptime percentages) are not consistently included for each
applicable role. For instance, at RA Creative, mentioning the percentage improvement in uptime after integrating the watchdog
service would provide clearer evidence of your impact. Adding such KPIs where appropriate will help align your experience
with industry expectations.

While technical skills are well-documented, direct links between these skills and measurable outcomes are limited outside of
the Deutsche Börse AG role. For example, describing how your expertise in API development or cloud infrastructure led to
specific efficiency gains or cost savings would strengthen your case. Consider adding statements such as “leveraged Docker
and Kubernetes to reduce deployment times by X%” for each recent position.

Relevant scope details—such as project budgets, deadlines met, or geographic coverage—are generally absent from your
recent work experience descriptions. For example, specifying the size of client organizations served at IDimitrov Ltd., or the
budgetary responsibility held during projects at RA Creative, would provide valuable context for the scale of your
achievements.
